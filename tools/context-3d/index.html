Units are metres. The site point is at 0, 0, 0 and north is +Y (OBJ is Y-up, so north is −Z there).

OBJ Rhino, Blender, 3ds Max (Rhino: keep "Map OBJ Y to Rhino Z" on). DXF 3D faces on layers Buildings, Terrain and Streets for AutoCAD, Rhino, Revit and SketchUp Pro. STL 3D printing and most modelers.

Data: buildings and heights © OpenStreetMap contributors; elevation from Copernicus DEM via Open-Meteo. Heights are often missing in OSM, so check them before you rely on them.
