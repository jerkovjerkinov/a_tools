Site Climate

A year of hourly weather for your site, turned into the charts and design clues a site analysis needs.

1Location

Tap the map to place the site.

2Data

Key figures

–Mean temperature
–Daily range (mean)
–Warmest month, mean max
–Coldest month, mean min
–Heating degree days (18 °C)
–Cooling degree days (24 °C)
–Annual precipitation
–Solar radiation, horizontal

Data: Open-Meteo historical weather API (ERA5 reanalysis, about 25 km grid). Good for site analysis, not for engineering design.

Pick a site and press "Analyse climate"Temperature, sun, wind and rain charts will appear here.
