Site Section

Cut a section through your site: terrain with spot levels, the buildings you cut in black, and the buildings behind as an elevation. Draw across a street for a street section, along it for a street elevation.

Tap the start (A) and the end (B) on the map. Drag A or B to adjust. Up to 1 km.

DXF is in metres: x along the section from A, y is height above sea level. Layers: TERRAIN, CUT, BEYOND, ROADS, LEVELS, TEXT.

Buildings and streets © OpenStreetMap contributors; heights come from OSM tags (height, levels × 3 m) or the default. Terrain: Copernicus DEM (90 m) via Open-Meteo, so small level changes and retaining walls do not show. Flat roofs.
