Metres, heights above sea level, north is +Y. Layers: Terrain, Contours, Water, Roads, Buildings, Vegetation, Analysis (flood band under 2 m above water, catchments, sea level rise by 2100). The .stl is a closed block in millimetres at the chosen scale, ready to slice.

The plan is drawn to scale with named layers for Illustrator or Inkscape. PDF opens the print dialog: choose "Save as PDF" and the paper size shown.

GeoTIFF in WGS 84 (EPSG:4326): metres above sea level, slope in degrees, height above the nearest stream or water in metres. GeoJSON and the shapefiles hold contours, roads, water, land cover, buildings with heights, trees, flow lines, flood bands (1, 2, 5 m), catchments and sea level rise, with attributes. The .kmz shows the plan, buildings in 3D and the analysis outlines in Google Earth.

Build a model to see the plates.

Red cuts, blue scores the outline of the next plate for gluing in register, black engraves the plate number. Check the files before cutting.

Data: elevation © Mapterhorn (Copernicus GLO-30 and national lidar) or Terrarium tiles (AWS); © OpenStreetMap contributors; heights GHS-BUILT-H R2023A, EC JRC; Copernicus Sentinel-2 via Earth Search; water history JRC Global Surface Water; sea level rise IPCC AR6 WGI Table 9.9. Trees inside woods and from satellite are placed by rule, not measured.

Pick a site and press "Build model".Ground, roads, water, trees and buildings come from open data in about half a minute.
