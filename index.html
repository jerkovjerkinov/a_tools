a_tools

Site analysis in minutes, not days.

Free design tools for architects and students. Pick your site once; every tool uses it. No install, no plugin, no Grasshopper.

Tools

Site Analysis Sheet

One click, one A3 board: figure-ground, sun path, wind rose, temperature, rain and sun, key figures and design notes. Print it or save as PDF.

NewA3 boardPDF

Sun & Shadow

Draw your building on the map and see its shadows and its neighbours' for any day and hour. Sun path diagram included.

LiveMapSVG · PDF

Site Climate

A year of hourly weather as temperature, comfort, wind rose, rain and sun charts, plus plain-language design clues.

LiveWind rosePDF

Zoning Envelope

Plot size, setbacks, coverage, FAR and height limit in; buildable footprint, floors and floor area out. TAKS / KAKS mode for Türkiye.

LiveFAR · EmsalSVG · PDF

Figure-Ground

The urban fabric around your site in black and white, with optional roads, water and parks. Grain and coverage figures included.

LiveUrban fabricSVG · PNG

Walkability

Streets reachable on foot in 5, 10 and 15 minutes, the daily needs within reach, and a 15-minute neighbourhood score.

LiveMapPDF

3D Context

Buildings and terrain around your site as a 3D model. Download OBJ, DXF or STL for Rhino, AutoCAD, Revit, SketchUp Pro, Blender or 3D printing.

New3DOBJ · DXF · STL

Sun Hours

Hours of direct sun on every square metre of ground for any day, with neighbours and your own building casting shadows. Checks the 2-hour rule.

NewMapPNG · PDF

Facade Sun

Hours of direct sun and solar heat on each facade through the year, with neighbours casting shadows. Shading advice per orientation.

NewMapSVG · CSV

Precedent Finder

Search built work by architect, type, period, style, country or distance from your site, with photos. Collect favourites on a printable A3 board.

NewWikidataPDF · CSV

Site Section

Draw a line to cut a section: terrain with spot levels, cut buildings in black, the buildings behind as an elevation, street H/W. True-scale A3, SVG and DXF.

NewSectionDXF · SVG · PDF

How it works

1 · Set your siteSearch a place once. Every tool opens there.
2 · Run the toolsEach tool is one page that works on phone and desktop.
3 · ExportDownload clean SVG diagrams or print a PDF sheet for your boards.
a_tools is in pilot. We are building it with architects and students. Tell us what worked, what broke and which tool you need next.
a_tools · open data from OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo (ERA5, Copernicus DEM). Results are for early design and education, not for permits or engineering.
